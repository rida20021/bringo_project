# Bringo Design System

## Icons
We use `lucide-react` for all iconography.
- Download: `<Download />`
- Open in New Page: `<ExternalLink />`
- Filters: `<Filter />`
- Expand/Collapse Menu: `<ChevronDown />`
- Print: `<Printer />`

### Icon Sizing
- Sidebar navigation items: `16`
- Sub-menu items and expand/collapse chevron: `14`
- Toolbar actions (download, print, filter, open in new page): `18`

### Navigation Icons
- Tableau de bord: `<LayoutDashboard />`
- Supervision (parent menu): `<Eye />`
- Contrôle des montants: `<Calculator />`
- Collectes & Dépôts: `<HandCoins />`
- Marchés: `<Store />`
- Équipe: `<Users />`
- Mobile menu toggle: `<Menu />`

## Brand Colors
- Primary: `#4a397e` (Deep Purple — Sidebars, Headers, Text)
- Accent: `#eab818` (Gold — Buttons, Highlights)
